Ancient Architecture

Roman Theatre, Thugga

Posted on January 1, 2023

Like all the other Roman cities of North Africa from the reign of Augustus, Dougga had its own theatre. According to historians, the monument was built between 168 and 169. It is located in the eastern part of the archeological site Dougga,[1] and can host over 3,500 spectators. A dedication engraved into the pediment of the stage and on the portico the dominates the city says the building was commissioned and paid for by P. Marcius Quadratus, and the dedication was celebrated with "scenic representations, distributions of life, a festival, and athletic games".
